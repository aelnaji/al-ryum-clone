# Al Ryum Group — 3D Scroll Cinematic: Video-Generation Prompt Catalog

> **Purpose:** corporate site footage for a landscaping + construction company — a
> showcase of **completed achievements**, filmed like real architectural /
> documentary cinematography.
>
> **Motion + clarity reference:** `moto-card.com`. What we take from it is *not* the
> advertising tone — it is the **movement discipline** (slow, singular, continuous
> camera moves) and the **image clarity** (tack-sharp, clean, no grain, no blur).
>
> **How it works in the site:** each generated clip (5s, image-to-video) is converted
> to a WebP frame sequence and scrub-scrolled (forward + reverse) as the user scrolls.
> So every prompt asks for **one continuous, steady camera movement** — no cuts, no
> text, no morphing. The clip is a single camera pass over real built work.

---

## Motion + clarity spec (the moto-card qualities, distilled)

**Movement — every clip:**
- ONE camera move, ONE direction. Never "zooms in and out" or "reverses".
- Slow and deliberate — the frame glides, it never rushes or stutters.
- Smooth, stabilized, weightless glide (no handheld shake, no sudden pans).

**Clarity — every clip:**
- Tack-sharp focus, high detail, crisp edges.
- Clean and minimal framing, generous negative space.
- No film grain, no motion blur, no lens smudge, no glow/over-bloom.

**Never:**
- No cuts, no jump cuts, no multiple shots.
- No text, no logos, no watermarks, no morphing, no flicker.

## Hard rules (every single clip)

- `"5 seconds, no cuts"` in every prompt.
- `"no text, no logos, no morphing, no flicker"` in every prompt.
- `"tack-sharp focus, high detail, no grain, no motion blur"` in every prompt.
- Name a lens (24/28/32/35/50mm) and a **natural** time-of-day light.
- Image ref = the **first frame** of that shot.

`assets/frames/{project-slug}/`. The scrub scripts auto-detect the frame count, so
you only need to drop the folder and refresh.
